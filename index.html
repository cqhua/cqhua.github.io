Biography

I am a Professor at the School of Computer Science, Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My work sits at the intersection of wireless communications and cyberspace security.

I have published more than 150 papers in leading international journals — including IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications, IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security, IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing and IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications — as well as flagship conferences such as IEEE INFOCOM, ICML, WiSec, ICC and Globecom. I have authored one English-language research monograph and hold more than 20 granted international PCT and domestic invention patents.

I have served as principal investigator on more than 20 national research programmes and industry collaborations, including the National Key R&D Program, the National 863 Program and projects of the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC). I currently lead the NSFC Shipping Joint Fund Key Project “Key Technologies for Wide-Area Maritime Ship Communication in the VHF Data Exchange System (VDES)” (2026–2029), which targets the fundamental theory and key technologies for next-generation maritime communication systems in wide-area coverage and capacity enhancement, efficient spectrum utilisation, and security resilience. I also lead the NSFC General Project “Neural Collapse based Source-Free Open-World RF Fingerprint Identification” (2027–2030), which addresses AI-empowered 6G physical-layer security and investigates RF fingerprint identification mechanisms for open, dynamic environments.

Research Interests

My research covers 6G networks and the satellite Internet, focusing on AI-driven intelligent wireless networks and endogenous security technologies, including but not limited to:

  • AI-driven efficient networking and transmission for the satellite Internet — dynamic resource scheduling for LEO constellations, inter-satellite cooperative communication, and interference-aware management.
  • Zero-trust security mechanisms for the satellite Internet — continuous authentication over satellite–terrestrial links, distributed access control, and satellite–terrestrial cooperative security defence.
  • Blockchain-based trusted security and distributed trust for satellite–terrestrial convergence — decentralised trust anchors built on consortium chains, DID and smart contracts, cross-domain dynamic authorisation, threat-intelligence sharing, and tamper-proof auditing.
  • Satellite–terrestrial converged network architecture and protocols — seamless handover in heterogeneous networks, satellite–terrestrial cooperative transmission, and quality-of-service assurance.
  • Intelligent spectrum management and dynamic sharing — spectrum situational awareness, intelligent interference avoidance, multi-domain cooperative spectrum scheduling, and low-altitude UAV spectrum monitoring.
  • AI-native 6G physical-layer security — deep-learning-based RF fingerprint identification, physical-layer key generation, and wireless channel feature authentication.
  • Wireless AI security and adversarial defence — defence against poisoning attacks on wireless AI models, adversarial example detection, and robust learning theory.
